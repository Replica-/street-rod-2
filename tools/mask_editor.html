Line: drag from end to end (Shift: straight / 45°). Rectangle, Ellipse: drag corner to corner, filled (Shift: square / circle). Size sets the brush, eraser and line thickness.
Saves to your Downloads: name_mask_1.png, name_mask_2.png… (white = change), name_request.txt (every touch-up with its description) and name_marked.png (the picture with each touch-up in its colour). Then tell Claude they are saved.

Wheel = zoom, right-drag or Space + drag = pan. The eye hides a layer while you paint another.
